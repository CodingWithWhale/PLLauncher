PLLauncher — PRIVACY POLICY
Version 1.0 - August 2026

1. NO DATA COLLECTION
PLLauncher does not collect, store, upload, or sell any personal data. There are no analytics, no telemetry, and no tracking.

2. LOCAL STORAGE ONLY
All of your settings, keybinds, tasks, schedules, time limits, and usage statistics are stored only in local files on your own computer. This data never leaves your device.

3. INTERNET ACTIVITY
The only internet activity is an optional check for updates against the PLLauncher GitHub repository. You can decline any update, and the software keeps working offline.

4. THIRD PARTIES
PLLauncher does not share data with any third party. The update check and download use GitHub (owned by Microsoft).

5. CHANGES
This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document.
